Nordica Unleashed 120

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The Unleashed 120 is not accused of being fragile. It is accused of being a lot of ski: fast, big, demanding, and not especially interested in lazy tree noodling.

Fit / stance / demand

Hard work in tight places

Reviews say bumps, tight trees, and low speeds require effort. This is not the ski that does the work for you.

Fit / stance / demand

Needs speed and space

It comes alive going fast. Technical areas demand strong legs, energy, and intent.

Conditions / performance

Not a daily resort ski

Reviewers frame it as a deep-day tool, not a practical everyday resort ski. Its best days are big snow days.

Fit / stance / demand

Not as freestyle as it looks

Despite the twin-tip appearance, reviewers say it does not naturally want park, switch, or casual jibbing. It is more charger than toy.

Noise, not conviction

One edge-separation complaint appeared for the broader Unleashed line, not clearly the 120. The agent did not find a wave of Unleashed 120 owner complaints.

Hokkaido verdict

Medium-high risk unless you are a strong skier who wants fast deep-snow lines. For lower-angle Hokkaido trees and playful slashing, it may be more tiring than fun.

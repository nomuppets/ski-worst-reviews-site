Salomon QST Echo 106

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The QST Echo 106 is a touring-biased compromise. It descends better than many light skis, but the complaints say it is heavy uphill and still not a real inbounds charger.

Conditions / performance

Heavy for touring

Reviewers call it near the upper end of what makes sense for dedicated human-powered skiing. The uphill tax is real.

Conditions / performance

Not locked-in at speed

It lacks the damp, straightline confidence of burlier resort skis. Do not expect it to smash resort chop like a heavy freeride ski.

Fit / stance / demand

Soft tips and tails

The flex may be an issue for bigger or more aggressive skiers. It is forgiving, but that comes with limits.

Conditions / performance

Not a deep-powder specialist

In deep powder it can bog down and lose liveliness. Wider skis are the obvious answer for real storm days.

Noise, not conviction

No tail skin-clip notch annoyed some people. Cosmetic topsheet and tail wear appeared, but the agent did not find a real delam or edge-failure pattern.

Hokkaido verdict

Moderate risk. Solid for Hokkaido touring and mixed sidecountry. Not the dream tool for resort Japow or deep tree days if powder is the whole point.

Atomic Maverick 115 CTI

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The Maverick 115 CTI is a powder charger with a narrow use-case. The beef is simple: it may be too directional and not fun enough for the tighter, softer side of Japan.

Conditions / performance

Not versatile

Reviewers and an owner framed it as a great powder ski but not something they love using without fresh snow. It is not a broad daily-driver tool.

Fit / stance / demand

Long-feeling and hard to release

Tester language points to a straighter, longer, more committed ski. It can feel like a tanker, not a speedboat, especially in tight terrain.

Fit / stance / demand

Less playful than alternatives

Some testers preferred narrower or more playful skis. The 115 was described as bulkier, stiffer, and less easy to make fun at slower speeds.

Fit / stance / demand

Build expectations are confused

Some comments expected a damp charger and instead found it surprisingly light or floppy. That mismatch matters if you are buying it blind.

Noise, not conviction

One tester mentioned hooky tips. One reviewer wondered about sidewall fragility, but the agent did not find repeated breakage or delamination complaints.

Hokkaido verdict

Do not buy it as your only Hokkaido ski unless you specifically want an open-terrain directional charger. For tight trees and slarvy Japow, there are safer shapes.

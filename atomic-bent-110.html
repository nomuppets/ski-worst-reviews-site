Atomic Bent 110

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The Bent 110 is fun when the snow is kind. The ugly part is what happens when Japan gets tracked, firm, or chopped: the light playful thing becomes the nervous thing.

Conditions / performance

Gets knocked around

Expert reviews and owner comments repeatedly say it lacks dampness and stability in chop, crud, tracked snow, and variable resort conditions.

Conditions / performance

Chatter on hardpack

Owners complain about chatter on groomed, icy, or hard-packed terrain. Replies often reduce the diagnosis to the same point: soft, light, wide skis do not love firm snow.

Manufacturing / durability

Durability concerns

The agent found multiple delamination and warranty complaints around Bent skis, including Bent 110 reports. It is not universal, but the signal is real enough to count.

Fit / stance / demand

Centered stance requirement

The ski wants a centered, upright skier. If you drive tips hard and expect a damp inbounds platform, you may hate the compromise.

Noise, not conviction

A tip-shape asymmetry complaint appeared, but it looked isolated. Mount-point gripes are mostly fit and style, not defects.

Hokkaido verdict

Moderate-to-high risk as a primary Hokkaido trip ski. Great if you want playful pow and trees. Weak if you need one dependable ski for resort chop, firm traverses, and aggressive speeds.

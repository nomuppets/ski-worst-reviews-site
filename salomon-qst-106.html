Salomon QST 106

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The QST 106 is the sensible pick in the room. Its sins are boring but real: firm snow, high-speed crud, and not enough float when Hokkaido properly turns on.

Manufacturing / durability

Weak on scraped firm snow

Reviewers say it falls short on very firm or icy groomers. Edge engagement can feel vague, chattery, or require commitment.

Conditions / performance

Not a true charger

In rough chop and crud at speed, it is not the dead-stable metal ski some aggressive skiers want.

Fit / stance / demand

Newer version can be more demanding

The stiffer profile and tail may make recent versions less forgiving in bumps, trees, and for intermediate skiers.

Fit / stance / demand

Not freestyle-focused

It is more directional than jibby. Switch landings and freestyle priorities are not its reason to exist.

Noise, not conviction

One measured pair showed notable weight variance. The agent did not find a recurring durability-failure pattern.

Hokkaido verdict

Moderate-low risk. A good mixed Hokkaido resort and tree ski for an advanced skier, but not the ultimate storm-depth powder tool. Bring or rent wider if it is nuking.

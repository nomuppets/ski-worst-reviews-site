Salomon QST Blank / S/Lab QST Blank

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The Blank is credible for Japan, but the worst complaints are about personality, not competence. It is damp, substantial, and not the effortless surfboard some people expect from a wide powder ski.

Conditions / performance

Not the float king

Reviewers and owners complain that it is not the easiest, driftiest, or floatiest ski for its size. It can handle powder, but it does not automatically smear and plane like a more dedicated soft-snow shape.

Fit / stance / demand

Heavy and muted

The same dampness that makes it composed also makes it less lively. People call it heavy, less energetic, harder to throw sideways, and not especially playful.

Fit / stance / demand

Can feel sluggish in tight terrain

In trees, bumps, and technical lines, the weight shows up. Some skiers found it cumbersome or slow to react, especially in longer lengths.

Conditions / performance

Firm snow is not its friend

The Blank is wide and soft-snow biased. Icy sections and true hardpack expose it, and several comments frame it as too wide for a one-ski daily driver.

Noise, not conviction

There was a water-ingress complaint surfaced in search, but the agent did not find enough readable evidence to treat it as a pattern.

Hokkaido verdict

Not a red flag, but not the safest Japan pick if the mission is effortless low-angle tree surfing. Good for a stronger skier who wants resort powder and chop composure. Less ideal if the dream is loose, easy, slashy Hokkaido snow.

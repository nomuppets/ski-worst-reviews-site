Atomic Bent Chetler 120

Manufacturing / durability Conditions / performance Fit / stance / demand

The Bent Chetler 120 is the obvious Japow fantasy ski. The beef is that fantasy skis become awkward when reality contains tracked resort snow, groomers, and icy exits.

Conditions / performance

Bad in chop

Reviewers and users repeatedly say it gets bucked, deflected, and speed-limited once fresh snow is cut up. It is not a crud-busting charger.

Conditions / performance

Too specialized

It is praised for deep days, but many complaints call it one-dimensional. It is overkill unless you have a quiver or a deep forecast.

Conditions / performance

Firm snow is ugly

It does not carve well, feels sluggish on piste, and sucks on ice by the standards of narrower or stronger skis.

Fit / stance / demand

Mount point sensitivity

The near-center mount can feel too far forward for directional skiers. Some reviewers advise moving back if you drive the shovels.

Noise, not conviction

A delamination complaint exists, but the agent did not find enough to call it a Bent Chetler 120 epidemic. Touring-tail annoyances are minor.

Hokkaido verdict

Good dedicated Hokkaido deep-snow ski. Risky as a one-ski trip tool. Take it when the forecast is deep or bring a narrower backup.
